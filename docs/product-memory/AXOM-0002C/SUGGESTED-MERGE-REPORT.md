# AXOM-0002c — Suggested Merge Report

## Result

The proposal layer references 66 of 196 Candidate Records and compresses
their foundation-level facets into 35 decision packages. This does not merge,
delete, or disposition any archival record. The AXOM-0002b.1 archive remains
the complete provenance source.

## Merge rules used

1. Merge only when evidence repeatedly describes one enduring product
   concept.
2. Extract only the relevant facet from a composite Candidate.
3. Preserve every conflict and historical classification.
4. Do not merge a principle, system, implementation behavior, defect, and
   research branch merely because they appeared in one checkpoint.
5. Treat ratified governance and AX-0009 definitions as authorities to cite,
   not text to duplicate.

## Deliberately unmerged

- Dashboard and Command Brief remain separate because one is the working
  surface and one is a defined decision system.
- Question Bank, Question Import, and Quiz remain separate because they own
  library/practice context, intake/trust, and runtime/history respectively.
- Blueprint, Course Tracker, Course Central, and Knowledge Graph remain
  separate because their ownership and source boundaries differ.
- Journal remains separate from Reports and Learning Analytics because
  reflection is not reducible to measurement.
- AI’s product role remains separate from optional execution-model research.
- Daily Games remains separate from navigation debt; visibility policy should
  not redefine the ecosystem.
- Local Vault/Recovery remains separate from accounts/sync research.
- Generic constitutional principles remain in AX-0002 rather than being
  duplicated as backlog records.

## Provenance protection

If the Product Owner later approves a merge:

- all source Candidate IDs should remain in the canonical record’s evidence;
- unresolved conflicts must remain explicit in Decision History;
- no Candidate ID becomes a permanent product identifier;
- non-foundation facets return to later disposition instead of disappearing.
