checkpoints often discussed a product idea, its implementation, and its debt
together. The following boundaries prevent those composites from becoming
overbroad canonical records.

## Proposal-level split decisions

These proposals are intentionally presented with an explicit Owner split
choice:

- `FOUND-003`: connected-product vision versus exact pillar taxonomy.
- `FOUND-011`: Local Vault versus portable recovery as one or two foundation
  records.
- `FOUND-012`: Reflection and Learning Evidence as one pillar or two.
- `FOUND-015`: current Question Import versus future Universal Import Engine.
- `FOUND-016`: one Quiz/Review system versus later artifact and mode records.
- `FOUND-018`: Course Tracker requires a distinct definition from Course
  Central.
- `FOUND-022`: one Reports/Analytics system versus separate general Reports
  and Question Analytics.
- `FOUND-029`: one answer-trust contract versus separate readiness, unknown,
  correction-precedence, and verified structured-evidence records.
- `FOUND-030`: one domain-contract record versus three records that cite the
  existing Lexicon.
- `FOUND-033`: one platform debt theme versus separate storage architecture,
  quota proof, and failure-recovery records.
- `FOUND-034`: strategic accounts/sync research versus current containment
  bug, and later identity/sync/recovery/migration records.
- `FOUND-035`: one AI execution research package versus separate cloud-proxy
  and browser-model investigations.

## Doctrine that should not be split into duplicate backlog records

Generic restatements of `C-001` through `C-011` and AX-0010 remain
governance evidence only. A narrower foundation proposal should survive only
when it adds product-specific meaning beyond the governing rule.
