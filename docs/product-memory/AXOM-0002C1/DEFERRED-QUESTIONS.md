# AXOM-0002c.1 — Deferred and Outstanding Questions

## Boundary

This report contains only genuine Product Owner choices or follow-ups that
cannot be resolved mechanically from ratified authority and verified evidence.
It does not reopen settled principles such as Local First, learner-work
protection, false-ready prevention, or review of consequential AI behavior.

Batches 1 through 2 are resolved. Questions in Batches 3 through 7 remain
pending until their decision batch is reached.

## Resolved in Batch 1 — Identity and Audience

- **OQ-001 / `FOUND-001` — resolved:** The approved product class is
  **Learner-Owned Academic Operating System**. Local First remains referenced
  governing meaning rather than title text; “operating partner” is not the
  canonical title.
- **OQ-002 / `FOUND-002` — resolved:** The mission is **Transform Academic
  Overwhelm into Clear, Grounded Progress** and includes continuity across
  learning. “One grounded next move” is its primary daily expression, not the
  total mission.
- **OQ-003 / `FOUND-003` — resolved:** The connected, compounding vision is
  approved without a fixed system count. The historical six-system taxonomy
  remains evidence and institutional history, not a frozen future limit.
- **OQ-004 / `FOUND-004` — resolved:** The primary audience is
  high-performance health-professions learners, beginning with medical
  students. The learner remains the primary user and authority under
  institutional distribution. “High-performance” must describe the target
  context, aspirations, and design demands. It must not imply that struggling,
  recovering, inconsistent, overwhelmed, or currently underperforming learners
  are unwelcome. AXOM’s recovery and non-punitive principles remain
  applicable.

## Resolved in Batch 2 — Product Philosophy

- **OQ-005 / `FOUND-005` — resolved:** Optional competition may exist only
  when it is honest, bounded, learner-controlled, optional, and subordinate to
  learning. Competition must never distort learning truth or mastery
  reporting. Public rankings, leaderboards, and future game systems remain
  separate product decisions.
- **OQ-006 / `FOUND-006` — resolved:** Question-first, feedback-driven learning
  is the flagship learning engine, not an exclusive doctrine. Lectures, notes,
  flashcards, reflection, curriculum systems, and future learning modes remain
  fully supported where appropriate.
- **OQ-007 / `FOUND-007` — resolved:** AXOM may make progress claims only when
  the evidence, denominator, time boundary, provenance, and uncertainty
  justify them. Activity must not masquerade as mastery.
- **OQ-008 / `FOUND-008` — resolved:** Recommendations, defaults, reminders,
  and soft limits remain learner-overrideable unless doing so would compromise
  source truth, data integrity, security, privacy, assessment integrity, or
  another explicitly governed trust boundary. Protected trust boundaries are
  not bypassed through normal override behavior. Generic Power Without
  Complexity doctrine remains governed by the Constitution and UX Standards.
