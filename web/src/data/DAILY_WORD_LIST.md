# AXOM Daily Word list provenance

`general-1` is an original AXOM compilation of familiar five-letter English
words, assembled independently for this project. It is not copied or adapted
from any external game's answer or allowed-guess list, and AXOM claims no
affiliation with an external word-game publisher.

The answer subset favors common US spellings, excludes proper nouns, and was
manually reviewed to avoid slurs, offensive terms, and unusually obscure
entries. Common regional forms and plurals may appear only in the broader
allowed-guess list.

The answer order is part of the deterministic puzzle contract. Do not reorder,
insert, or delete answers under `general-1`; publish a new version identifier
for any such change. Bundled client-side words and derived answers are
inspectable. AXOM does not use misleading answer obfuscation.
